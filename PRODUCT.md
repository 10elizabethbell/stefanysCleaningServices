# PRODUCT — Stefany's Cleaning Services LLC

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages; Cal Sans from Google Fonts is the only external resource
- **Source:** Muse brief, 2026-10-20, plus the business's own Facebook page meta (facebook.com/stefanycleaningservices, read 2026-10-20)

## Users
- Homeowners in and around Maplewood, NJ who see the business on Facebook (group posts, the page) on their phones, often inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner (Stefany, inferred from the brand name; unconfirmed), who receives the site for free.

## Purpose
Give a cleaning business that lives only on Facebook a real home on the web: show who they are and what they clean, and turn a visitor into a Messenger conversation asking for a quote.

## Positioning
A local home cleaning service in Maplewood, NJ that gives every room extra attention ("Fall Calls for Extra Care"), so the customer gets a cozy, welcoming home.

## Capabilities
- Services: home cleaning; deep cleaning; seasonal cleaning (category from the brief; no itemised list published)
- Contact: Facebook Messenger only (m.me/stefanycleaningservices resolves to the Page's message thread). No phone, no email published.
- Quote form composes a message and opens Messenger, with a copy fallback.

## Constraints
- No phone or email anywhere: every contact path is Messenger; the code switches to text/call once `CONFIG.PHONE` is filled.
- Service area: only "Maplewood NJ" (the Facebook page's title). No towns list; don't invent one.
- No real work photos. The flyer's bathroom image is a design-template photo (inferred), not their work, so it is not used.
- No reviews, prices, hours, years in business, insurance claims: none on the page.

## Brand commitments
- Name as on the logo and ad caption: "Stefany's Cleaning Services LLC".
- Logo colours: brand blue #077dd4, leaf green #8cc63f.
- Voice: warm, seasonal, "we/our" (the flyer says "Our team").

## Evidence on hand
- Logo (Facebook profile image, 500×500)
- Ad caption text, 2026-10-20 (verbatim in the brief)
- Facebook page title "Stefany Cleaning Services | Maplewood NJ", 477 followers

## Principles
1. Phone first; the first screen shows who, what, where and the Messenger action.
2. Only facts from the brief, the flyer or their page.
3. One clear way to reach them, never two of the same control in view.
